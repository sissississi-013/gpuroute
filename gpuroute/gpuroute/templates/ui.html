Compute

Everything your agents can reach, and how busy it is right now. Agents see the same list through compute_map.

(SHARE A MACHINE)

Run this on any Mac or Linux box with a GPU. Jobs only start while it’s idle, each is capped at 30 minutes, and the owner sees every job.

uvx --from "git+https://github.com/sissississi-013/gpuroute#subdirectory=gpuroute" gpuroute share
gpuroute join name@ip:7400#tokenpaste what it prints

Jobs

Anything your agents ran: training, embeddings, transcription, benchmarks. Outputs come back to this machine.

gpuroute run my_script.pydeps are read from its imports

Spend

Agents hold an allowance, never your credentials. Paid jobs above the threshold wait for you, and the budget becomes the provider’s hard timeout.
